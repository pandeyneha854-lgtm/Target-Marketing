# TechGadget Inc. – Marketing Performance & ROI Dashboard | Power BI

## 1. Business Problem

TechGadget Inc. is facing a significant decline in overall marketing efficiency.

Over the past quarter, the company increased its marketing budget by 40%, but total revenue grew by only 12%.

Despite higher marketing investments, customer acquisition costs are increasing, and the company is struggling to identify which marketing channels and advertising creatives are generating profitable results.

The company needs a data-driven solution to identify budget wastage, improve campaign performance, and maximize marketing ROI.

## 2. Project Objective

The objective of this project is to develop an interactive Power BI dashboard that helps the Chief Marketing Officer (CMO) monitor marketing performance, identify inefficient campaigns, and make data-driven investment decisions.

## 3. Strategic Business Questions

### A. The Cost Crisis – Channel & Campaign Efficiency
- Which marketing channels are consuming the highest budget?
- Which campaigns have a high Cost Per Acquisition (CPA)?
- Which campaigns are exceeding their target CPA?
- Which channels are generating better results at lower costs?

### B. The Creative Disconnect – Creative Performance
- Are video ads converting better than image ads?
- Are high-budget video campaigns generating sufficient returns?
- Which creative formats have higher CTR and conversion rates?
- Are higher CPMs resulting in better campaign performance?

### C. The ROI Gap – Return on Ad Spend
- Which campaigns are achieving their Target ROAS?
- Which campaigns are failing to meet their ROAS targets?
- Which marketing channels are generating the highest ROAS?
- Where should the company optimize or reallocate its marketing budget?

## 7. Business Insights & Recommendations

The dashboard helps the marketing team:

- Identify campaigns with higher acquisition costs.
- Detect inefficient marketing channels.
- Compare video and image creative effectiveness.
- Identify campaigns that fail to achieve Target ROAS.
- Optimize marketing budget allocation.
- Improve overall marketing efficiency and profitability.

## 8. Project Outcome

This dashboard provides a comprehensive view of marketing performance and enables the CMO to identify cost inefficiencies, evaluate advertising creatives, monitor campaign profitability, and make informed marketing investment decisions.
